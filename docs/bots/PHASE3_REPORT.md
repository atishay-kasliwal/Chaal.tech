**What did not**
- Samay's moods (§14). The diagnosis stands (his curiosity margins dominate and moods acted on raises he never makes); the fix reached single decisions (tests) but not population behaviour. Next step: event-triggered quirks for Samay, measured directly, rather than more mood weight.
- Night-to-night variety dropped for Rohit, GG and Dhoni (§9). The search had no variety target; a session-variation band per character should be added before another run.
- Two Dhoni bands stay outside on every candidate (bluff index 0.43 vs ≤ 0.40), and the adaptation regression above means the next search must gate on scenario strength, not just population strength.
