What each fix did:

1. **Scenario-strength gate** (paired per table, 50 boots/100 slack). It worked as a filter: it stopped 6 of 144 candidates, mostly against the calling station. But at 24 tables × 200 hands, the station and maniac CIs are ±1000 to ±1800 boots/100 (section 12c), so the gate can only catch gross weakness. A stricter gate needs about 4× the scenario tables.
2. **Night-to-night variety gate** (≥ 60% of the ancestor's). It stopped 3 candidates in the search. The finals still lost variety for Samay (0.66 vs 1.08) and Dhoni (0.49 vs 0.68), because the search measures variety on 12 nights and the finals on 24. The 12-night estimate was too noisy to protect the floor.
3. **Samay's event-triggered quirks** (`stubbornStreak`, `onToYou` on `tp-dna@5`, with recency fading over 4 hands). The code works: unit tests show each quirk firing only in its moment and fading. But the two event bands are still flat (lossPackDeltaX -0.00, caughtBluffFoldDeltaX 0.00; section 3, and section 14's "mind adds" columns near zero). The moments are rare (seen, under pressure, within 4 hands of the event), and at strength 0.6 the push is too small to move a population rate. Making the bands move would mean stronger quirks or a wider trigger. That is a behaviour change, so it is left for your call.

Suggested next step, if you want one: a Phase 3.2 that (a) quadruples the scenario and night samples inside the gates, and (b) tries Samay's event quirks at strengths 1.0 to 1.5 with the trigger widened to "facing any bet". Nothing here has been published, merged or seeded.
